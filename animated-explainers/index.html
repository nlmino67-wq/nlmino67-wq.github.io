A workflow you can repeat

The first video sets everything up. Every one after it is faster.

  1. 1

    Characters

    Designed once as reference sheets, so every scene starts from the same face, outfit and style.

  2. 2

    Storyboard

    Each line of the script becomes a still. You approve the look and the jokes before anything is animated.

  3. 3

    Animate

    Approved stills become shots. Several takes each, and the weak ones are thrown out.

  4. 4

    Cut

    Edited to your voiceover for timing, with captions and sound effects, then exported for each platform.
