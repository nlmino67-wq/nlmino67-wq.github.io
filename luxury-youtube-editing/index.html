How the tenth video stays as good as the first

You agree the style once. After that I work from the sheet.

  1. 1

    One style sheet

    Before the first edit we agree one page: pacing, caption style, graphics and music level. You sign it off once.

  2. 2

    Same templates

    Captions and graphics are built once as templates. Video ten uses the same ones as video one.

  3. 3

    Same sound chain

    Dialogue is cleaned and levelled the same way every time. Music sits under the voice.

  4. 4

    Checked before every draft

    I check each cut against the sheet before you see it. If something does not match, I fix it first.
