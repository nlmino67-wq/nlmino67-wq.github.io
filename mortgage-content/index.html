How I'd run your channels

Up to 30 hours a week, from research to reporting.

  2. 2

    Research and calendar

    I study the brokers and finance creators doing well in Australia, track rate news and lending changes, and turn it into a month of planned posts and scripts.

  3. 3

    You approve the scripts, I edit and post, answer comments and forward enquiries. Each month you get a short report on followers, views and leads.
