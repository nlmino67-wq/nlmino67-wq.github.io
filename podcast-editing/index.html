How I handle a two-camera podcast

Sync, cut, switch, finish.

  1. 1

    Sync

    Both cameras and every mic file lined up by waveform, then checked at the start, middle and end for drift.

  2. 2

    Cut

    False starts, repeats, dead air and off-air talk removed. The meaning and the rhythm of the conversation stay.

  3. 3

    Switch

    Angles follow who is speaking and who is reacting. No switching for its own sake.

  4. 4

    Finish

    Levels balanced between speakers, noise reduced where needed, colour matched across both cameras.
